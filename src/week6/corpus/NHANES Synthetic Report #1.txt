Executive Summary
This synthetic NHANES demographic report provides a comprehensive overview of population characteristics for a nationally representative sample of U.S. residents. Demographic data form the backbone of NHANES weighting and interpretation, influencing every downstream domain including nutrition, chronic disease, laboratory biomarkers, and environmental exposures.
The synthetic dataset includes 10,000 respondents and reflects realistic U.S. population patterns. Key findings include socioeconomic disparities, age related differences in household composition, and notable variation in insurance coverage across demographic groups.
1. Survey Description
The demographic component of NHANES is collected through structured household interviews. This synthetic dataset mirrors the NHANES design:
•	Civilian, non institutionalized U.S. population
•	Oversampling of minority groups
•	English and Spanish administration
•	Household roster and socioeconomic modules
Core Variables Included
•	Age
•	Sex at birth
•	Gender identity
•	Race/ethnicity
•	Household size
•	Housing tenure
•	Education
•	Income to poverty ratio
•	Insurance coverage
•	Citizenship and country of birth
These variables support weighting, stratification, and subgroup analysis.
